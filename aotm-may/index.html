About Arena Animation

Arena Animation proudly celebrates 30 years of excellence in vocational training and creative education. Since 1996, Arena has empowered thousands of students with industry-ready skills in Animation, VFX, Gaming, Web Design, and Digital Media. Established by Aptech Limited, Arena Animation continues to shape future creative professionals through industry-focused training, expert guidance, and practical learning. With a strong commitment to innovation and career development, Arena helps students build successful careers in the growing AVGC-XR industry. Driven by creativity, teamwork, resilience, presentation skills, deadline management, and strong industry exposure, Arena Animation remains dedicated to transforming passion into profession.
